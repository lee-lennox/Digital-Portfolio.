**Result**: I discovered that I’m passionate about software development and want to grow into a backend developer or full stack developer. This guided my decision to focus more on Java, databases, and web development in my portfolio and future learning.

## Personality Assessment

### Evidence

I explored my personality traits using free online quizzes and self-reflection through YouTube content and articles on personal development. I didn’t do a formal test like MBTI, but I identified patterns in how I approach problems and work with others.

From this informal assessment, I found that:
- I enjoy solving complex problems, especially through coding.
- I prefer structured environments but also value creativity.
- I am naturally curious and enjoy learning new technologies.
- I am both introverted (focused, independent) and capable of working well in teams when needed.

These traits align with roles like:
- **Software Developer**
- **Backend Engineer**
- **Cybersecurity Analyst**
### Reflection (STAR Technique)

**Situation**: I needed to understand my personality better to guide my career planning and teamwork approach.

**Action**: I watched self-assessment videos on YouTube, completed online personality quizzes, and reflected on my study and project habits.  
**Result**: I discovered that I work best in structured, problem-solving roles where I can focus deeply, such as software development. This insight helps me choose projects, work styles, and career paths that align with my strengths.
